Quick reference

Check evidence against the question.

A passage can be true, on topic, and correctly cited while answering the wrong question. Shipping duration does not establish shipping cost.

  1. Read what the question asks for.
  2. Check whether the current passage supplies that information.
  3. Return the decision and reason from the evidence-check node.
  4. Let the router generate for accepted evidence, retry rejected evidence within budget, or use fallback at the limit.

Lesson 10 supports two exact shipping questions with separate phrase rules. Unsupported questions are rejected. This is a teaching rule, not a general validator. Adding relevant evidence and improving the check solve different parts of the workflow.
