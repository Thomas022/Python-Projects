generation_allowed

No hit, unsupported question, or insufficient passage under the lesson's narrow rules. Inspect reason to distinguish these.

The cost checker accepts an explicit price per order. It rejects a passage saying costs vary, even though retrieval finds that passage. General question answering needs more coverage than these two question forms.
