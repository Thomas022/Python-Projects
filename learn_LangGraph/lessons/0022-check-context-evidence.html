Why did shipping insurance reach context_ready in Lesson 21?

Check your recall

The retriever found the word shipping and the formatter assembled its passage. Neither step checked whether that passage answered an insurance question.

1 · Add a decision between retrieval and preparation

load_documents → retrieve → check_evidence
accepted → prepare_generation → END
rejected → fallback → END

The Python example keeps the same files and word-overlap retriever. A new check writes evidence_ok and reason. The conditional edge reads evidence_ok to choose the next node. Primary reading: conditional edges.

This teaching checker deliberately supports only the normalized questions cost and receipt. Cost needs a passage stating a shipping price per order; receipt needs the returns receipt requirement. Other question forms are rejected. It is a small explicit rule, not a general understanding of evidence.

2 · Follow the state

  1. After retrieve, cost has shipping paragraph 2 in hits. Insurance has shipping paragraph 1. A hit alone allows neither question through the gate.
  2. After check_evidence, cost has evidence_ok=True; insurance has evidence_ok=False, with an unsupported-question reason.
  3. The router sends cost to prepare_generation, which formats cited context. Insurance goes to fallback, leaving context empty.

generation_allowed means this checker accepted the evidence for a supported question. No model has generated an answer yet. An eventual model answer will still need its own check before release.

Use the original files, with the shipping price restored to $5. Predict evidence_ok and status before each run:

Existing terminal exports override .env settings; use a fresh terminal if old exports conflict.

Check after predicting and running

Cost and receipt are accepted and reach generation_allowed. Warranty has no retrieved hit. Insurance has a hit but is outside the supported question forms. Both reach evidence_rejected through fallback, for different reasons.

4 · Make evidence fail without changing the question

Temporarily change shipping.txt paragraph 2 to Standard shipping costs vary per order. Run cost again. The retriever still finds the word cost, but the checker rejects the passage because it lacks the required price. Restore Standard shipping costs $5 per order. afterward.

This shows the gate checks both the question and passage. Insurance is blocked by our restricted question scope; its result alone would not prove the checker understood insurance. A broader assistant needs broader evidence rules and evaluation cases. Official retrieval overview.
